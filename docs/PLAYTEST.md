# Playtest kit

## Open the game
- Run `pnpm dev` and open the printed address (or open the shared single-file build / hosted link).
- On the home screen pick **Quick game**. Add `#play` to the URL to skip the home screen, `#lab` for the Map Lab.
- Options shows the version number (1.0 plus 0.01 per merge into `main`).

## Five scenarios (plus a phone game)
1. **First 30 turns.** Quick game, follow the hints. Found a city, explore, pick research, build a few things. Is it clear what to do next?
2. **A war.** Pick a fight with a neighbour (or let one start). Try attacking, defending, taking a city and making peace.
3. **Trade and diplomacy.** Send trade routes, make deals, meet city-states and send envoys, try the congress.
4. **A religion game.** Found a pantheon and a religion, spread it, see what rivals do about it.
5. **An online game with a friend.** Play online, host a lobby, share the code, play a few turns, drop and rejoin.
6. **On a phone.** Play a few turns in a phone browser. Can you reach everything, and is the text readable?

## Feedback questions
1. Where were you confused?
2. Where were you bored?
3. Where were you frustrated?
4. Where were you delighted?
5. How long did a turn feel (too short, fine, too long) at turns 10, 50 and 100?
6. Did anything feel unfair (AI, combat, events, loyalty)?
7. Did anything feel broken or look wrong?
8. What is the one thing you'd change first?

## Reporting a bug
Send: the version (Options), browser and version, device, the game seed, what you did and what you expected, and an exported save (game menu, **Save**).
